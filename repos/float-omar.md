## What this repo is

`float-omar` holds Omar's personal financial data, family capital planning, source document archives (raw statement PDFs, CSVs, indices), non-engineering forensic reconciliation materials, and divorce tracing packages. It was split out of `khamel83/float` on 2026-07-24. The reconciliation engine, PWA, database (`float.db`), migrations, web server, and application pipelines remain in `float`.

Data flow between repositories is governed by the 5-category data contract defined in `CONTRACT.md`:
- **Category A (Local-only)**: Machine-local state (`float.db`, `private_paths.local.json`, `omar/canonical/`, `omar/statement_index/extracted_text/`) resides only in `float`.
- **Category B (Vendored input)**: Data authored here (`omar/input/`, `omar/statement_index/`, `omar/finance/`, statement PDFs) is vendored into `float` under hash-verified SHA-256 manifest integrity checks (`npm run verify:float-omar-vendor`).
- **Category C (Development tooling)**: Production scripts operating on `float.db` belong in `float` (`scripts/legacy-omar-tools/`) or are retired.
- **Category D (Generated reports / Static snapshot)**: Archived outputs (`account_master.csv`, `wealth_walk_rows.csv`, `plaid_items_raw.json`, 106 historical backup files) retained as static snapshots.
- **Category E (Bidirectional planning)**: Planning assumptions (`family_capital_assumptions.json`) and Python models (`retirement_model.py`, `monte_carlo_sorr.py`, `tranche_model.py`) are authored here and vendored into `float`; `FAMILY_CAPITAL_MASTER.md` is generated by `float` (`scripts/generate-family-capital-plan.ts`) and published back here.
- **Category F (Vendored narrative)**: Narrative markdown (`omar/thoughts/`, `omar/ACCOUNT_MASTER.md`) authored here and vendored into `float`.

The domain model enforces source-anchored double-entry Event ledgers derived from raw source bytes (statement PDF > Plaid posted > Mint/Copilot > pending). Amounts originate only from the Raw layer, verified by per-account statement-period rollforwards (`opening Anchor + Σ(Events) = closing Anchor`, residual 0) and investment market-term identities (`contributions − withdrawals + market_change = Δbalance`).

## What is actually built

### 1. Data Product & Local Store Subsystem (`src/data-product/v1/`)
- `sync.ts` / `sync.test.ts`: Client data product synchronization engine.
- `local-store.ts` / `local-store.test.ts`: Local storage persistence abstraction for client state.

### 2. Reconciliation & Forensic Script Pipeline (`scripts/`)
- **Financial Truth & Accepted Ledger**: `build-omar-financial-truth.ts` combines `account_identity`, time-ranged `account_ownership`, current balance sources, and canonical statement identities into deterministic registers (`financial_universe.csv`, `identity_crosswalk.csv`, `source_coverage.csv`, `observation_freshness.csv`, `opening_balance_sheet.csv`, `material_exceptions.csv`). `build-accepted-ledger.ts`, `publish-accepted-ledger.ts`, `build-acceptance-gate.ts`, and `build-canonical-financial-records.ts` handle accepted ledger generation and publication.
- **Divorce Tracing & Proof Packages**: `build-divorce-tracing-report.ts`, `build-divorce-data-package.ts`, `build-ciprari-proof-package.ts`, `compare-ciprari-theories.ts`, `compute-ciprari-trace.ts`, and `vti-counterfactual-analysis.ts` execute closed, source-backed separate property allocation walks for Betterment and Fidelity X63 accounts through date of separation (DOS).
- **Rollforward & Non-Asset Reconciliation**: `reconcile-rollforward.ts`, `apply-non-asset-reconciliation.ts`, `reconcile-non-asset-transactions.ts`, `reconcile-bank-pool.ts`, and `reconcile-pairing-engine.ts` handle transfer pairing, sign-rule verification, and non-asset bank/card rollforwards.
- **Source Indexing & Attribution**: `build-source-file-index.ts`, `generate-source-of-truth-map.ts`, `generate-input-manifest.ts`, and `generate-per-row-tieout.ts` build document manifests and map raw observation rows to source locators.
- **Phase Processors & Parsers**: Python and TypeScript extractors across Phase 6 (`scripts/phase6/` for Ally deduplication, Amex registry, retirement anchors, USC flows, crypto), Phase 9, Phase 10 (Amex extraction/diff), and Phase 11 (`phase11_extract.ts`, `phase11_migration.ts`), alongside statement parsers (`parse-ally-monthly-statements.py`, `patch-pdf-classification.py`).

### 3. Family Capital Planning Engine (`omar/planning/`)
- **Configuration (`family_capital_assumptions.json`)**: Governs spending targets ($144,000/year after-tax spending capacity target, $120,000/year guardrail floor), success horizon (age 90), terminal liquid reserve ($750,000 for high-acuity care), children's IDGT commitment ($300,000 reserved, backed by $1.5M term insurance), base retirement date (February 12, 2035), ongoing working additions (pretax 403(b) max, $20,000 employer 401(a), $24,000 taxable investing), Monte Carlo success threshold (90%), and deterministic real return bands (3%, 4%, 5%, 6%).
- **Simulation Calculators**: `retirement_model.py` (deterministic annual cashflow model), `retirement_definitive.py`, `retirement_visual_proof.py`, `monte_carlo_sorr.py` (sequence-of-returns risk Monte Carlo simulator), and `tranche_model.py` (three-pool asset bucketing).
- **Narrative Baseline**: `FAMILY_CAPITAL_MASTER.md` serves as the generated authoritative planning reference.

### 4. Canonical Data Packages & Forensic Corpus (`canonical/` & `docs/forensics/`)
